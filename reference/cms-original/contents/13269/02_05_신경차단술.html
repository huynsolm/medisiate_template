Epidural Neuroplasty

Neuroplasty is that relieves pain during procedure for patients with severe pain.

It is a treatment using an anesthetic and a small amount of steroids, which is usually performed when the pain is severe depending on the patients' condition.

Epidural Neuroplasty

When is it treated?

  • In case of severe pain, neuroplasty may be done to relieve the pain depending on the patients.

  • This procedure is mainly performed for patients with back pain and radiating pain in legs.
    Moreover, it can be done to all recurrent chronic musculoskeletal disorders that are difficult to treat.

  • It is an advantage that there are no sequelae and complications but it must not be treated simultaneously with prolotherapy.
