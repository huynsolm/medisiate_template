PDRN (PolyDeoxyRiboNucleotide)

PDRN therapy for chronic tendons injuries and Osteoarthritis or degenerative joint disease

The treatment is called DNA injection, and it is a DNA complex that is extracted and purified from trout sperm and made as an injection. It is also commonly used for skin care and it is now attracting more attention as a proliferative agent for prolotherapy.

PDRN (PolyDeoxyRiboNucleotide)

What are the benefits of PDRN therapy?

  • 1

    The therapy is a cell reactivation effect, and it acts as a tissue regeneration activity by increasing cell regeneration and tissue composition production.

  • 2

    It shortens the wound healing time, helps the production of collagen tissue proteins, and promotes the differentiation of various sub-cells such as stem cells/fibroblasts/myeloblasts/chondrocytes.

  • 3

    It has the effect of improving local blood circulation by inducing the production of VEGF(Vascular endothelial growth factor).

  • 4

    Since it has high affinity for platelets, it is easy to apply to the wound or regenerated area.
