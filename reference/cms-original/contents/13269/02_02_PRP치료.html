PRP - Platelet Rich Plasma Therapy

What is PRP treatment?

When the body is injured, healing-regulating growth factors appear to heal the wound and protect the body, which are abundantly contained in platelets.

PRP (Platelet Rich Plasma) Therapy repairs degenerative joints by separating and concentrating only platelets with your own healing-regulating growth factors.

Why is PRP Therapy

RECOMMENDED FOR YOU

  • Sports Injuries

  • Injuries from daily activities

  • Patients who want a non-surgical treatment

  • Musculoskeletal pain and inflammation due to aging

PRP Treatment

NOTICE

  • The entire PRP procedure from start to finish takes 30 minutes or so.

  • In general, the pain during the PRP injection is minimal.

  • Since the patient's own blood is used, there is little chance of side effects such as infection or allergy.

  • Upon completion of PRP injection, it is acceptable for you to continue with your daily activities but excessive exercise on the day of treatment is not advised.
