How do we treat it?

  1. 01

    Treatment Mechanisms

    how to

    Prolotherapy is the way to create new tissue in weakened areas by stimulating the body's natural healing mechanisms.
    An appropriate inflammatory reaction is triggered by injecting a proliferative agent. Thereby, eventually, new tissue is created which strengthens the weakened structure.
    In addition, it is the way that rebuilds the damaged area to gradually recover by repeating Prolotherapy.

  2. 02

    Therapeutic procedures

    how to

    When human tissues are damaged, the healing process takes place through the 3 stages such as inflammatory phase, proliferative phase, regeneration and stabilization phase.
    Prolotherapy is the treatment process that artificially induces a recovery process in tissues such as ligaments and tendons.
    Ligaments basically prevent joint damage by blocking excessive movement of the joint.
    However, if the ligaments are strengthened through the damage recovery process of prolo treatment, the stability of the joint is more increased.
