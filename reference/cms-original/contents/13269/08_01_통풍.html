    Acute gouty arthritis

    Gout is a disease in which uric acid crystals are deposited in the soft tissues around joints due to an increase in uric acid blood level. A common initial symptom is due to 'acute gouty arthritis'.
    In most cases, it is most common in the big toe joint, and can also occur in the ankles, foot joint, and knee joints.

    Duration of flare-ups

    how to

    Even though you don't usually exercise, your joints suddenly become swollen or hot and painful is a common symptom.

    Urate-lowering therapy

    In the case of chronic progression, it is recommended to perform uric acid lowering therapy immediately.
